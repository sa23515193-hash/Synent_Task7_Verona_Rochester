Since 1974

A fashion journey built around footwear.

Stylo's official story says its journey began in 1974 in Lahore under the name Bano Chapal. The brand has since grown into a large Pakistani fashion house with a focus on women's shoes and wider fashion categories.

The current official website highlights shoes, bags, jewelry, accessories, apparel and perfumes, with 200+ outlets across Pakistan.
